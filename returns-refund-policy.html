Returns & Refunds

Because Dancers Are Dope products are made to order, we do not accept returns or exchanges for an incorrect size or color, or for a change of mind.

Damaged or defective items

If your item arrives damaged, misprinted, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 30 calendar days of delivery. Include your order number and clear photos of the item and the problem.

Eligible issues may qualify for a replacement or refund. You do not need to return a damaged or defective item unless we specifically instruct you to do so.

Address or delivery problems

If an order is delayed, lost, or returned because of an incorrect or incomplete address, contact us with your order number so we can review the available options. Additional shipping charges may apply to a replacement shipment.

Contact

Email hello@dancersaredope.com to report an issue. Please keep your order number and photos available.
