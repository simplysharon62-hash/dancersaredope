Shipping Policy

Dancers Are Dope products are made to order through our print-on-demand fulfillment partner. We currently ship orders to addresses in the United States.

Production time

Orders typically take 2–7 business days to be produced before they are shipped. Production and delivery estimates are estimates and may vary because of product availability, holidays, order size, or carrier delays.

Delivery time

After production, standard U.S. delivery typically takes about 2–5 business days. Tracking information is provided when the order ships.

Address accuracy

Please review your shipping address carefully before completing checkout. An incorrect or incomplete address may cause a delay, return to sender, additional shipping charges, or a partial refund.

Questions

For order questions, email hello@dancersaredope.com and include your order number.
